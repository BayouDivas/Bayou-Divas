Good To Know

Quick Questions

A few details to help dancers, event organizers, and potential partners get started.

How do I ask about joining the team?

Email bayoudivasllc@gmail.com with the subject “Bayou Divas Team Interest.” We'll respond with current details and next steps.

How can I ask about booking the Divas?

Email the event date, location, type of event, expected schedule, and any other details you know. We'll follow up to discuss availability and fit.

How can a business support the team?

Businesses and organizations can email us to start a conversation about sponsorships, collaborations, or other potential support.
